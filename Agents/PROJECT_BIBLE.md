# MEMORIA ZERO

## Project Overview

Memoria Zero is a narrative-driven psychological sci-fi investigation visual novel set in 2071, where every human consciousness can be archived before death and later reconstructed as a digital continuation.

The player works for a government agency called Memoria Zero as an Identity Validator, a specialist tasked with determining whether a biological human or a digital duplicate is the authentic continuation of a person who has died, vanished, or been reconstructed.

The game combines investigative gameplay, moral ambiguity, existential horror, and emotionally charged branching storytelling.

Its core fantasy is simple and powerful:

The player is not merely solving a mystery.

The player is deciding what counts as a person.

---

# Core Concept

## Elevator Pitch

In a future where memory can be preserved and copied, the line between life, identity, and consciousness has become legally and ethically unstable.

As an Identity Validator, the player investigates alleged continuations of the dead, navigating encrypted archives, biometric evidence, personal testimonies and moral contradictions to decide whether a suspect is truly the same person.

# Lore and Worldbuilding

## The World of 2071

Humanity can now archive consciousness before death.

A neural backup can later be reconstructed into a continuation.

These continuations preserve memory but not necessarily identity.

---

## The Authority of Continuity

Governments, corporations and private organizations compete to define personhood.

Questions include:

- inheritance
- citizenship
- rights
- criminal responsibility
- legal existence

---

## The Role of Memoria Zero

Memoria Zero is the agency responsible for validating identity continuations.

Its mission is determining whether reconstructed consciousnesses are authentic continuations or sophisticated imitations.

---

## The Black Box Problem

Science can measure:

- neural resonance
- memory consistency
- emotional patterns
- behavioral continuity

But science cannot definitively prove identity.

The final judgment is always human.

---

## The Social Consequence

Society is divided.

Some continuations are accepted.

Others are rejected.

The world no longer agrees on what a person is.
