# MEMORIA ZERO

# Visual Identity

## Visual Style

- Retro-futurist minimalism
- No cyberpunk neon
- Clinical architecture
- Controlled visual language
- Bureaucratic elegance

---

## Color Palette

Primary:

- White
- Pale Grey
- Steel Grey

Accent:

- Crimson

Contrast:

- Black

---

# Key Symbols

## Authentication Ring

Represents:

- validation
- authority
- ritual
- identity

The Authentication Ring is the core visual symbol of the franchise.

---

## Red Case File

Represents:

- danger
- truth
- consequence

---

## Iris

Represents:

- observation
- uncertainty
- consciousness

---

# Identity Validator

## Design Goals

The player character must become iconic.

---

## Requirements

- Recognizable silhouette
- Strong cosplay appeal
- Instantly identifiable
- Marketable design

---

## Visual Elements

- Long white-grey coat
- Authentication Ring necklace
- Minimal biometric mask
- Crimson accents

---

## Silhouette Rule

The character must be recognizable even in shadow.

---

# Iris Expansion

## Iris

- governmental
- elegant
- oppressive
- professional
