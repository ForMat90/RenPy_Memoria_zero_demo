# MEMORIA ZERO

# Investigation System

## Core Gameplay Loop

1. Receive a case
2. Review case materials
3. Examine evidence and testimonies
4. Compare biometric and memory data
5. Identify inconsistencies and contradictions
6. Form an authenticity judgment
7. Deliver a verdict
8. Observe consequences

---

## Investigation Modes

### Witness Interviews

Dialogue-based investigations.

The player gathers testimony and evaluates credibility.

### Subject Validation

The player directly examines disputed continuations.

### Archive Review

Examination of memory fragments and archived consciousness data.

---

## Gameplay Philosophy

The tension comes from:

- interpretation
- uncertainty
- moral ambiguity

The tension never comes from:

- combat
- reflexes
- action sequences

The player should feel intellectually engaged.

---

## Player Agency

The player is not selecting dialogue.

The player is deciding:

- what evidence matters
- who deserves trust
- which definition of identity should prevail

---

# Authenticity System

## Core Principle

Authenticity is not binary.

It exists on a spectrum.

---

## Evaluation Dimensions

### Biometric Continuity

Biological consistency.

### Behavioral Consistency

Actions and habits.

### Memory Coherence

Internal memory stability.

### Emotional Resonance

Authentic emotional patterns.

### Social Recognition

Relationships and public identity.

### Moral Legitimacy

Ethical implications.

---

## Verdict Types

### Authentic Continuation

Legally validated.

### Probable Continuation

Strong evidence exists but uncertainty remains.

### Inconclusive Continuation

Insufficient certainty.

### Counterfeit Continuation

Rejected identity claim.

---

# Evidence System

## Evidence Categories

- Personal Testimony
- Biometric Records
- Neural Scans
- Archived Memories
- Administrative Documents
- Witness Statements
- Corrupted Files
- Emotional Logs

---

## Evidence Philosophy

Evidence should always create doubt.

Evidence should rarely create certainty.

---

## Reliability System

Evidence may be:

- Accurate
- Incomplete
- Manipulated
- Forged
- Misinterpreted

---

# Choice System

## Design Rules

The case contains:

- Trust Choices
- Evidence Choices
- Moral Choices
- Institutional Choices

---

## Choice Consequences

Choices must affect:

- later dialogue in this case
- trust values
- which ending plays

The variables used in the script are:

- `trust_sarah`
- `prova`
- `domanda`
- `purga`
- `verdetto`

## One Memorable Character

A character players remember.

## One Moral Dilemma

No obvious correct answer.

## One Twist

A revelation that changes perspective.

## One Irreversible Choice

A meaningful commitment.

## One Cliffhanger

An unresolved question.
