# MEMORIA ZERO

# Sound Design Guide

## Audio Direction

Audio must be:

- Minimalist
- Precise
- Atmospheric
- Oppressive
- Controlled

Silence is a design tool.

Silence is not empty space.

Silence creates tension.

---

## Core Sound Elements

### Climate System Hum

Low ambient environmental hum.

The world should always feel active.

---

### Mechanical Clicks

Subtle confirmation sounds.

Used for:

- UI
- Validation
- Documents
- Navigation

---

### Ventilation Noise

Very subtle background ambience.

Used to reinforce institutional environments.

---

### White Noise

Used sparingly during:

- memory corruption
- archive analysis
- continuity anomalies

---

### Authentication Chimes

Signature sound of the franchise.

Associated with:

- Iris
- Validation
- Authentication Ring

---

# Musical Direction

## Composition Style

- Ambient
- Minimal
- Sparse
- Psychological

Avoid:

- Epic music
- Heroic music
- Action music

---

## Emotional Goal

The soundtrack must create:

- uncertainty
- isolation
- reflection
- existential discomfort

---

# Accessibility System

## Core Philosophy

The entire game must be playable without sight.

Accessibility is mandatory.

Accessibility is not optional.

---

# Blind Accessibility

## Full Narration Mode

Every piece of content can be narrated.

Includes:

- Menus
- Documents
- Choices
- Dialogues
- Evidence
- Reports

---

## Narrated Interface

Every menu entry must be readable.

Example:

"Case Archive."

"New Evidence."

"Continue Investigation."

---

## Narrated Documents

Every document can be fully read aloud.

Example:

IRIS:

"Death Certificate."

"Subject Daniel Voss."

"Status: Deceased."

"Backup Archive Located."

---

## Audio Feedback

All important interactions require sound feedback.

Examples:

- Evidence found
- Menu selected
- Choice confirmed
- Case completed

---

## Navigation

Supported Inputs:

- Mouse
- Keyboard
- Controller
- Touch

---

# Audio Investigation Mode

## Description

Alternative gameplay mode.

Evidence is consumed primarily through narration.

Players can investigate without visual dependency.

---

## Features

- Full document reading
- Voice-guided navigation
- Audio evidence review
- Spoken menu hierarchy

---

# Voice Profiles

Every major character should define:

- Vocal tone
- Speech rhythm
- Speech complexity

---

# Audio Goal

Players should feel:

"There is always something listening."

Even in silence.
